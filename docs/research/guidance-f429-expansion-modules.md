# GuidancePilotF429 expansion: sensor and storage requirements

Research date: 2026-11-04. AI-assisted investigation for [GuidancePilotF429: establish sensor and storage electrical requirements](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/issues/8), part of [GuidancePilotF429: integrate external IMU, GPS and microSD for bench use](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/issues/6). Source review is pinned to `7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146`. Scope is STM32F429I-DISC1, ArduPlane, bench only. This document establishes constraints; it does not approve a connector pinout, power hookup or mounting rotation.

## Findings

The existing drivers support both MPU variants and u-blox 7. ChibiOS supports microSD over SPI or its native SD controller. No new component driver is indicated by the named parts. Carrier identity and power evidence remain prerequisites for a wiring schematic and board-definition changes. The supplied module names alone do not establish their connector voltages or pin order.

### MPU9250 versus MPU6500

| Bare component | Electrical and interface constraints | Sensor content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MPU9250 | VDD 2.4–3.6 V; VDDIO 1.71 V through VDD. I/O follows VDDIO. I2C up to 400 kHz; SPI all-register access 1 MHz, sensor/interrupt reads up to 20 MHz. | Accelerometer and gyro plus a separately addressed internal AK8963 magnetometer; no barometer. |
| MPU6500 | Detailed DC table specifies VDD and VDDIO 1.71–3.45 V. The overview says 3.6 V; use the tighter table limit for design pending manufacturer clarification. I2C up to 400 kHz; SPI all-register access 1 MHz, sensor/interrupt reads up to 20 MHz. | Accelerometer and gyro; no integrated compass or barometer. |

Sources: TDK [MPU9250 product specification rev 1.1, §§3.4, 4, 7–8](https://invensense.tdk.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/PS-MPU-9250A-01-v1.1.pdf), [MPU6500 product specification rev 1.3, §§1.2, 3.3, 4, 6](https://invensense.tdk.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/PS-MPU-6500A-01-v1.3.pdf). These are chip requirements, not permission to feed an unidentified carrier's VIN with those voltages. A regulator can impose input headroom; a separate VDDIO rail or translator can change header logic levels.

For I2C, hold nCS high, strap AD0 deliberately to choose 0x68 or 0x69, and verify SDA/SCL pullups go to the intended I/O rail. For SPI, provide SCK, MOSI/SDI, MISO/SDO and an independent active-low nCS; AD0/SDO is then an output and must not be hard-strapped as an address input. Check whether the carrier fixes nCS high or omits SDO before selecting SPI. The electrical design should establish deselected CS levels during MCU reset; an external pullup is a design choice to check against existing carrier resistors, not an assumed fitted part. Follow the chip's decoupling and unused-pin circuit; a photo cannot prove all connections. Sources: [MPU9250 specification, §§4.1–4.3, 7–8](https://invensense.tdk.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/PS-MPU-9250A-01-v1.1.pdf), [MPU6500 specification, §§4.1–4.3, 6–7](https://invensense.tdk.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/PS-MPU-6500A-01-v1.3.pdf).

Identity must be verified at register 0x75: MPU6500 returns 0x70; MPU9250 returns 0x71. These are not the I2C slave addresses. The MPU9250 register description has an inconsistent reset-value heading (0x68), but its explanatory text and ArduPilot use 0x71. The internal AK8963 has its own identification check, so a successful gyro/accelerometer probe does not prove a working compass. Sources: TDK [MPU6500 register map §4.38](https://invensense.tdk.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/MPU-6500-Register-Map2.pdf), [MPU9250 register map §§4.38, 5](https://invensense.tdk.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/RM-MPU-9250A-00-v1.6.pdf), ArduPilot [WHOAMI constants](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_InertialSensor/AP_InertialSensor_Invensense_registers.h#L184) and [AK8963 identity check](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_Compass/AP_Compass_AK8963.cpp#L232).

The common [Invensense driver](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_InertialSensor/AP_InertialSensor_Invensense.cpp#L105) accepts I2C and SPI devices and recognizes both identities. It polls the FIFO through a periodic callback. The [data-ready helper](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_InertialSensor/AP_InertialSensor_Invensense.cpp#L505) reads a status register when no DRDY pin is configured: **INT is optional for this driver**, not a required extra wire for the proposed first integration.

MPU9250 compass support is a separate [AK8963 backend](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_Compass/AP_Compass_AK8963.cpp#L86): SPI uses the IMU auxiliary bus; I2C uses the bypass path established during IMU initialization. A board definition must deliberately declare the compass probe and correct MPU9250 instance. [Sparky2](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_HAL_ChibiOS/hwdef/sparky2/hwdef.dat#L117) illustrates the separate `IMU Invensense` and `COMPASS AK8963:probe_mpu9250` declarations. It is an implementation example, not this board's wiring.

Both drivers accept `Rotation`; the inertial driver [applies it to gyro and accelerometer](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_InertialSensor/AP_InertialSensor_Invensense.cpp#L421), and AK8963 independently calls `set_rotation`. Supported predefined rotations are listed in [rotations.h](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_Math/rotations.h#L25). Select rotations only after chip axes, carrier orientation and intended board forward/top are known; the compass declaration need not use the same value as the IMU. Do not infer `ROTATION_NONE` from silkscreen text.

### u-blox NEO-7N-0-002

The manufacturer's datasheet explicitly covers this type number. Bare NEO7N VCC is 2.7–3.6 V; digital input operating range is 0–VCC, with VIH at least 0.7×VCC. It is not a 5 V UART module. Factory UART is 9600 baud, 8N1, with NMEA messages active and UBX accepted as input. Published supply sizing includes 67 mA maximum, separate from typical acquisition/tracking values; carrier LEDs, regulator losses and antenna supply are additional. Source: [NEO-7 datasheet UBX-13003830 R07, applicability table, §§4.2–4.3, 8](https://content.u-blox.com/sites/default/files/products/documents/NEO-7_DataSheet_(UBX-13003830).pdf).

For a normal ArduPilot UART connection, GPS TX goes to MCU RX and MCU TX goes to GPS RX, with common ground and verified logic compatibility. Both directions are needed for default automatic startup configuration. Current [AP_GPS](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_GPS/AP_GPS.cpp#L78) cycles candidate baud rates and sends initialization data; its default u-blox blob requests binary output at 230400 baud ([blob definition](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_GPS/AP_GPS_UBLOX.h#L49)). An option supports 115200 instead. The [detection gate](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_GPS/AP_GPS.cpp#L767) means receiving factory 9600 NMEA alone is not equivalent to working default UBX initialization. The [UBX backend](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_GPS/AP_GPS_UBLOX.cpp#L1814) explicitly handles u-blox 7; no new NEO7 driver is indicated. Verify the real receiver's firmware/configuration later rather than assuming its retained settings are factory defaults.

PPS/TIMEPULSE is optional: u-blox says leave it open if unused; ArduPilot's PPS path is [compile-time guarded](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_GPS/AP_GPS_UBLOX.cpp#L1258). UART operation requires the NEO interface-selection state, which a carrier may already establish. Bare-module backup supply, unused USB supply and antenna circuits must follow the [hardware integration manual, §§2.5–2.7, 3.2](https://content.u-blox.com/sites/default/files/products/documents/MAX7-NEO7_HardwareIntegrationManual_%28UBX-13003704%29.pdf). Identify active versus passive antenna and any bias supply. The metal-can label does not identify the carrier's VIN regulator, battery circuit, UART level shifting or connector order.

### Unidentified MicroSD Board v1.02

This silkscreen is not sufficient identification. A conventional bare card uses a 2.7–3.6 V supply; do not apply 5 V to its supply or signals. SPI uses clock, MOSI, MISO and card-select; native four-bit SD uses CLK, CMD and DAT0–DAT3. A carrier exposing only the SPI subset cannot provide the native four-bit connection without modification. Source: ST [AN5595, MMC/SD SPI interface and initialization](https://www.st.com/resource/en/application_note/an5595-spc58xexspc58xgx-multimedia-card-via-spi-interface-stmicroelectronics.pdf). Native bus naming here refers to the STM32 SDIO peripheral, not an assumption that this is an SDIO-function card.

ArduPilot's [hwdef generator](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_HAL_ChibiOS/hwdef/scripts/chibios_hwdef.py#L891) enables FATFS for native SDIO or a named `SPIDEV sdcard`; the [runtime implementation](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_HAL_ChibiOS/sdcard.cpp#L37) uses four-bit native mode or MMC-over-SPI. Therefore SPI is already supported and does not require adding a storage driver. [OmnibusF4Pro](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_HAL_ChibiOS/hwdef/omnibusf4pro/hwdef.dat#L111) illustrates a named SPI card device with initialization and operating clocks; choose this carrier's validated maximum later. An independently controlled, reset-safe CS is required for a shared SPI design, along with verification that every deselected device releases MISO.

Waveshare sells a superficially relevant [Micro SD Storage Board](https://www.waveshare.com/product/micro-sd-storage-board.htm) supporting SPI and SDIO and offering a schematic through its [wiki](https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/Micro_SD_Storage_Board). **No evidence connects that product to the user's v1.02 board.** Its specification must not be substituted for the unidentified carrier. Regulator presence, VIN headroom, translators or resistor dividers, MISO behavior and pullup values/rails remain unknown. Card model and current demand also remain unknown; do not budget its write current using an IMU's few-milliamp typical current.

## Minimal future firmware scope

The pinned [GuidancePilotF429 hwdef](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_HAL_ChibiOS/hwdef/GuidancePilotF429/hwdef.dat) defines SPI5 for the onboard gyro with LCD deselected, USB, USART1 and flash parameter storage. It declares no complete IMU, external GPS UART or SD card. USART1 is the stock ST-LINK VCP, not an unallocated GPS port ([board README](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_HAL_ChibiOS/hwdef/GuidancePilotF429/README.md)).

After carrier and board pin evidence is reconciled, the likely changes are a chosen SPI/I2C device and Invensense probe with verified rotation; a separate AK8963 probe only for a confirmed working MPU9250 compass; a free UART in `SERIAL_ORDER`, configured as GPS; and either a named SPI card device or a complete native SDIO declaration. [Serial manager](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/blob/7a7939c2f533e22b88e0deca42c81d6590daa146/libraries/AP_SerialManager/AP_SerialManager.cpp#L37) gives early serial slots MAVLink defaults; adding a UART does not itself make it a GPS port. Existing flash parameter indices/storage need no change merely to add log storage. These are prospective changes, not a reviewed patch or build result. The existing missing-barometer behavior remains relevant; none of the named bare parts supplies one.

## Evidence required before a final schematic

1. Sharp front/back photos of **each complete carrier**, showing every connector label, PCB manufacturer/revision, component top marking, jumper and solder bridge; seller/manufacturer link and matching schematic if available. Provide the IMU chip marking as well as the marketed name.
2. IMU exposed CS, SDO/AD0, INT and axis labels; regulator/translator markings; actual VDD/VDDIO connections, pullup values/rails and any permanently strapped interface pins. A photo may need schematic or unpowered continuity confirmation to resolve hidden traces.
3. GPS complete metal-can marking and carrier markings; connector pin order; regulator/translator and backup battery circuitry; antenna model, active/passive type and bias supply. Confirm both UART directions are accessible.
4. SD carrier pin labels and circuitry, especially whether DAT1/DAT2/DAT3/CMD are exposed, versus only SPI; regulator/translator markings; card make/model/capacity. State if the socket includes card detect; do not confuse its CD switch with the card's DAT3/CS signal.
5. Intended mounting top/forward direction and physical chip axes; proposed cable lengths and an unpowered assembled-wiring photo before physical assembly and the power plan are confirmed.
6. Intended supply source and USB connections, rail/current ratings and any regulator datasheets. Map power paths and ground before measuring unloaded and loaded rails after physical assembly and the power plan are confirmed. [GuidancePilotF429: integrate external IMU, GPS and microSD for bench use](https://github.com/Mohameddewedar/ardupilot/issues/6) records earlier approximately 2.86 V ST-LINK / 2.92 V estimated VDDA and intermittent native USB failures. Those readings do not establish capacity, regulator headroom, ripple or transient margin for the expansion. Do not infer permission to power the modules from nominal 3.3 V compatibility.

## Validation and limits

Reviewed official TDK and u-blox electrical/interface documents and the pinned source paths above. Requested PDF table screenshots to cross-check electrical tables; the browser exposed textual PDF tables, while one MPU9250 screenshot request failed content-type resolution. TDK's separate download endpoint returned HTTP 403; the official indexed PDF text and newer MPU6500 rev 1.3 table corroborate the stated limits. No vendor identity was established for the SD carrier. Documentation whitespace is checked with `git diff --check`. No compilation, upload, wiring, powered measurement, sensor detection or hardware test was performed. Human carrier evidence and assembly confirmation remain outstanding; this research does not establish normal ArduPlane initialization or flight readiness.
